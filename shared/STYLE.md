# Razzle visual language

Rules for authoring slides (by hand or by an agent) in the style of the example decks
(`examples/TEE Presentation.pdf`, `examples/Who-Watches-The-Watchers-2.pdf`). All numbers are
canvas px on the PPTist 1000 × 562.5 canvas. Reusable pieces are in `shared/library.json`
(generated by `shared/tools/build_library.mjs`; constructors in `shared/tools/kit.mjs`).

## Principles

1. Few words. A slide carries a title, a picture, and at most a handful of short phrases.
   The speaker says the sentences; the slide shows what they are about.
2. A glyph per sentence. Each sentence of the talk gets a small picture that cues it
   (a pentagon of nodes for "distributed", a sinking boat for "sinks", a lock for "private").
   Use the `glyphs` category; reveal one glyph per build step.
3. Build by fading, not by hiding. Everything that will be on the slide is usually visible
   from the start in faded form; each click brings the next part into full colour.
4. Flat shapes. No shadows, no gradients, no rounded corners on content boxes, no icons from
   outside the library. White background.

## Fonts

- Libre Baskerville: everything (titles, body, labels). Regular, bold for numbered steps and
  panel headings, italic for annotations.
- Roboto Mono: code, on `#f3f3f3`.
- Inter: small sans labels only in the Basis Blog theme.

Font files (latin, woff2) and `@font-face` rules: `shared/fonts/` (`fonts.css` declares the
families `Libre Baskerville`, `Roboto Mono`, `Inter`). Previews load the same families from
Google Fonts. Use these exact family names in `defaultFontName` and `font-family`.

## Layout

- Title: text element at left 34, top 48, width ≤ 930 (PPTist's 10 px inset puts the ink at
  x = 44, baseline near y = 91). `textType: 'title'`.
- Left ink margin 44; right ink margin ≥ 34. Content occupies y ≈ 115 to 530.
- Diagram left, words right (list at x ≈ 488), or diagram centred with annotations around it.
- Vertical rhythm: list items 60 apart; numbered steps 127 apart (y ≈ 156 / 283 / 410);
  stacked layers 61 high with 27 to 28 gaps.
- One idea per slide. A sequence of builds on one slide is preferred over many similar slides.

## Builds

- Every slide has `turningMode: 'no'`. Builds are PPTist `animations`; each click-triggered
  animation is one step, `meantime` animations join it. Use `fadeIn` / `fadeOut`, duration 300.
- Fading list: each item is its own text element in `#cccccc`. For each step, place a
  `#595959` (or `#000000` for steps) copy at the identical position, fade it in on click, and
  fade the previous dark copy out in the same step (`meantime`).
- Fading diagram: draw the faded version underneath (faded network / gray layers) and fade the
  full-colour parts in on top. To push things back, draw the full version on top and fade it
  out over the faded copy.
- Step 0 should already make sense on its own.
- Speaker metadata: `razzle.script[i]` is what is said at step i; `razzle.cues` lists spoken
  phrases that advance (`action: 'step'` at `atStep`, or `'next'` on the last step).

## Diagram idioms

- Agents: filled triangle (PPTist `triangle` formula), 2 px black outline. Teal agent, blue
  counterpart agent, orange human. A person with their agent: teal triangle with an orange
  triangle inside sharing its base (`human-node`). Name labels in gray 16 to 19 above or beside.
- Networks: nodes on a regular polygon (pentagon, hexagon), gray 2 px edges drawn beneath the
  nodes. Highlight a pair with a translucent blue dashed region behind it and a blue italic
  annotation outside it.
- Boxes: flat `cutRectDiagonal` boxes (keypoint 0.18: top-right and bottom-left corners cut),
  no outline, black centred label. Layer stacks: verified layer on top in `#77c7d0`, trusted
  layers in `#eeeeee`, the layer under discussion in `#4285f4`, the broken one in `#cd8484`.
- Boundaries: a heavy dashed `#595959` line (width 3) across the diagram with an italic gray
  label just above its right end ("Proof boundary"). A dark-red vertical rule with an italic
  gray label to its left marks a group of layers ("Trusted Computing Base").
- Pipelines: stage boxes joined by 2 to 2.5 px `#595959` arrows; a long pink runtime bar
  underneath; dashed drop lines from a stage to a "Bug here ⇒ / consequence" callout.
- Frames: dashed `#4a86e8` rounded frame for a group; dashed teal cut-corner "document" frame
  for an artefact (title + inner gray box).
- Conversations: two agents far apart, `message`-shape bubbles between them stacked 44 apart,
  teal with the tail down-left for the left party, blue mirrored (`flipH`) for the right party.
  Projected local states: teal bubble plus pale gray `[Wait for …]` boxes.
- Numbers: one big number centred, a gray line under it, the punchline number in dark red.
- Results: large cut-corner panels (`#dcf0f2` with teal text; `#eeeeee` with black heading
  and bold-italic gray verdict).
- Code: Roboto Mono 15 on `#f3f3f3`, keywords `#4285f4`, the faulty line `#980000`; keep
  indentation with no-break spaces (PPTist collapses ordinary spaces).

## Annotations

- Italic Libre Baskerville placed next to what it describes, never with an arrow if proximity
  suffices.
- Blue `#4285f4`: names a region or a protocol ("Ad-hoc protocols", "Private calendars").
- Teal `#0097a7`: a claim about the artefact ("Formally verified. But what are we trusting?").
- Gray `#595959`: labels and asides ("Proof boundary", "Each stage is an attack surface.").
- Dark red `#980000`: the failure ("There was a bug!", "wrong program").

## Cue glyphs

80 × 80 pictures built from PPTist shapes and lines: flat palette fills, 2 px black outline.
Available: network, sinking boat, lock, key, scale, magnifier, bug, shield, check, cross, clock,
database, server, document, gear, lightning, cloud, person, warning, cycle, funnel, light bulb,
hourglass, target, flag, eye, layers, globe, exchange, chat, two agents, human with agent,
rocket, trend up, fuzzer crab. Scale them to 100 to 120 px for a glyph row, with one italic
gray word under each; reveal one per sentence.

## Basis Blog theme

Alternate theme from the Ship Your Interpreter figures: background `#fffcfc`, ink `#311317`,
muted `#6e5d57`, cream boxes `#efe8db` (fixed inputs `#e0d4c1`) with a 1.5 px ink outline and
square corners, section frames outlined `#d8c9b4` with letter-spaced bold Inter headings,
terracotta `#dd7954` (outline `#b85c39`) for the one conclusion box and feedback arrows.
Box names in bold Libre Baskerville, details in Inter. Library items: `basis-*`.

## Element conventions

- Library items are positioned at (0, 0) with placeholder ids `<item-id>-<n>`; regenerate ids
  (and `groupId`) when inserting. Multi-element items share one `groupId`.
- Shapes made from a PPTist formula carry `pathFormula` + `keypoints` with `viewBox = [width, height]`.
- Fully transparent fills are `rgba(255,255,255,0)`.
- Preview a deck: `node shared/tools/render_preview.mjs decks/demo.json out.html --steps --png out.png`.
